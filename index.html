    How to read this

    • Solis AI (actual). What really happened, priced from the inverter's own counters of grid import, export and battery use.
    • Every cost is only money paid to the energy company for grid power. Battery wear and energy left in the battery are not paid to anyone, so they are not added in.
    • Export is off, so nothing here assumes selling to the grid.
